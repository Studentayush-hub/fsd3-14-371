Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common pairing, warranty, and firmware queries.

Press and hold the dedicated Auracast button on your AURA device for 3 seconds until the LED ring pulses violet. Open the AURA Control App on iOS or Android, tap "Scan Broadcasts," and join the desired audio stream instantly.
Ensure your Windows PC is updated to Windows 11 Build 23H2 or higher for native Bluetooth 5.4 LC3 codec support. Alternatively, plug in the included AURA Low-Latency USB-C Dongle to achieve guaranteed 20ms ultra-low latency.
All official AURA hardware includes a 24-month hardware warranty covering battery degradation below 80%, driver mismatch, and manufacturing flaws. Simply attach your proof of purchase in the support form above to initiate an expedited advance replacement.
Download the AURA Companion App on mobile or desktop. Connect your headset via Bluetooth, navigate to Device Settings > Firmware Update, and follow the OTA (Over-The-Air) flash guide. Updates take under 2 minutes.
