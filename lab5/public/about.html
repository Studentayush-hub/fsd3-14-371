Our Core Engineering Pillars

Design without compromises. Acoustics without limits.

Custom Bluetooth 5.4 PHY layer tuning reduces transmission delay down to 20ms, allowing real-time gaming and pro studio playback without desynchronization.

Precision DSP Acoustics
