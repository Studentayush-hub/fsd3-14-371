ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI.
 It can understand natural language and provide responses to questions and instructions.
  People use ChatGPT for learning, writing, coding, brainstorming, summarizing, and solving problems.
   It can explain complex topics in simple language and help users complete tasks more efficiently.
    ChatGPT is designed to interact with users through natural conversations and can be useful for students,
 developers, teachers, and professionals.
